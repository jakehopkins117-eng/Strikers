STRIKERS v3.7 — SELF-LEARNING AI

What it does
- Studies every graded prediction.
- Compares average model confidence with actual win rate.
- Tracks results for pitcher, bullpen, lineup, injury, home-team, high-confidence, margin, and sportsbook-value signals.
- Activates a small calibration correction after 40 graded games.
- Caps the correction at +/-2.5 probability points and never rewrites core model weights.
- Displays progress, factor lift, learned correction, and model takeaways on Model Performance.
